How I lead

Building teams, not heroes

  • I give room and trust

    I make the outcome, the risks and the boundaries clear. The team chooses the technical route.

  • I'm there when it matters

    Reachable, also outside office hours. And during an incident I check just as much whether everyone has eaten.

  • I don't stop at the first no

    If the goal is right, I find a way past rules and red tape, and take everyone along.

  • I say what I see

    Direct and clear. That's useful, even when it rubs.

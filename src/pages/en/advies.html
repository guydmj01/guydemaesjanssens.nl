Consulting

No vendor to sell. No legacy to defend.

I give family businesses, SMEs and law firms without their own IT department an honest outside view of their IT, and the simplest way forward.

Sound familiar?

Three things I see a lot

  • Your IT grew. Nobody planned it.

    One system at a time, one subscription at a time. Now nobody knows exactly what you have, what it costs and what depends on what.

  • Everything depends on one supplier, or one person.

    What if your IT person leaves tomorrow, or your supplier doubles the price? {{term:vendor-lock-in}} creeps in without anyone choosing it.

  • You have to ‘do something with AI’. But what?

    Everyone says you must. Nobody tells you what it gets you, or what happens to your data.

Why me

A big bank's experience, at your scale

  • IT for thousands of people

    At Rabobank, my teams and I built the workplace for about 10,000 people and the {{term:platform}} for more than 200 development teams. The same principles work at your scale, without the bureaucracy.

  • Choices that keep you free

    At the bank we built everything {{term:infrastructure-as-code}} and {{term:cloud-agnostic}}, so we were never stuck with one supplier. That's what I look for on your behalf too.

  • AI that stays in your building

    At home I run my own AI lab with {{term:local-ai}}. I show you what AI can do for your business, without confidential data going to the big cloud providers. For a law firm, that's where the conversation starts.

  • Listening first

    I come from hospitality. First I want to know what's going on, with management and with the people who use the systems every day. Only then advice, in plain language.

My story

From host to tech lead

I didn't study computer science. I come from hospitality. Looking back, that's what makes the difference.

  1. Achterhoek · The Hague

    The host

    I grew up in the Achterhoek, in the east of the Netherlands, and studied business administration at Hotel School The Hague. All those years I worked in hospitality. It teaches you one thing well: think about what your guest needs next. People are with you in their free time, so you make it their best time.

  2. Germany

    Data before IT

    For my graduation I worked for a large German hotel chain. I built a pricing model for several of their hotels, based on past bookings for rooms and events, plus holidays, local events and the weather. It let them adjust their prices every day. That's when I knew: I wanted the technical side, but right in the middle of the business.

  3. 2018 – 2022

    Starting on the shop floor

    An IT traineeship placed me at Rabobank. After a year the bank asked me to join them, in their Young Professional Programme. I started on the {{term:service-desk}}, moved to global {{term:hardware}} delivery and became {{term:scrum-master}} for several infrastructure teams. That's how I got to know IT the way users and teams really experience it.

  4. 2022 – now

    Selected to lead

    I was selected from a small group of high potentials for the bank's Tech Lead programme. Since 2022 I've been a {{term:tech-lead}}: first for the digital workplace of about 10,000 Rabobank employees, now for the {{term:platform}} more than 200 {{term:devops-team}} build on.

    My success doesn't come from being the best at what I do. It comes from building teams that are.

    About my work as a tech lead {{icon:arrow-right:18}}

  5. At home

    Learning by building

    Because I didn't study computer science, I put in extra time to really understand the technology. I do that by building. First home automation, then our home's energy use, now mostly AI.

    I built a golf coach app that gives feedback on a video of your swing. There are no AI models for golf swings, so I used a large {{term:pose-model}} as the teacher and {{term:knowledge-distillation}} that gives concrete feedback. And at home I run my own AI lab with {{term:local-ai}}: good enough for real work, without any data going to the big cloud providers.

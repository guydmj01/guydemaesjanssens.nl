Experience

The platform a bank's engineers build on

Strategy, delivery, risk, money and people. In a bank that can't afford to go down.

2024 – now

Tech lead, Digital Platform Foundation

Rabobank · the Netherlands

The Digital Platform Foundation provides the platform, standards, tooling and developer experience for more than 200 DevOps teams. When the Foundation gets better, teams across the bank deliver faster, safer and more consistently. My job was to make that happen, and to keep it running.

  • Strategy and roadmap

    Set the direction for the Digital Platform. Security, stability, continuity and technical risk were part of the plan from the start, not added at the end. Speed and new ideas, inside a regulated bank that has to stay up.

  • From separate tools to a self-service platform

    Moved the Foundation from individual technical capabilities to a reusable, self-service platform. Teams take common building blocks off the shelf instead of solving the same infrastructure and delivery problems again.

  • Governance, risk and reliability

    Kept resilience, security and compliance on every squad's agenda. Made sure technical priorities were acted on, tightened production and continuity processes, and kept operational reliability visible next to feature work.

  • Front-End Foundation

    Helped shape the strategy for one frontend ecosystem: consistent customer journeys, faster delivery, less lifecycle work and stronger security. It is now used across the bank and seen internally as a front-runner in Rabobank technology.

  • GenAI and agentic AI in software delivery

    Pushed the engineering agenda on DevSecOps, Internal Developer Platform principles, automation and developer experience. In 2026 I aligned Retail Tech's SDLC initiatives on GenAI and agentic AI: found the bottlenecks and steered towards shared building blocks instead of disconnected experiments.

  • Budget, vendors and people

    Took part in investment and budget decisions, business cases, vendor and tooling choices, workforce planning and sourcing specialists. My rule: test every investment against strategic value and reuse before committing money.

  • Leading through others

    Developed tech leads and engineers, helped teams take more ownership, and made priorities and responsibilities clear. Worked with architecture, product, business leadership and outside partners to clear the obstacles between teams and their results.
